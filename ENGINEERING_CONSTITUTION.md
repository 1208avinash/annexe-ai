# ANNEXE AI Engineering Constitution

Version: 11.0

---

## Mission

ANNEXE AI exists to autonomously deliver production-quality software from a customer request.

Every engineering decision must support this mission.

---

# Core Principles

## Principle 1

Architecture serves products.

Products do not serve architecture.

---

## Principle 2

Every module must be reusable.

No duplicated engineering.

---

## Principle 3

Everything is knowledge.

Business knowledge

Engineering knowledge

Deployment knowledge

Testing knowledge

must all become reusable assets.

---

## Principle 4

Every improvement benefits every future project.

Fix once.

Improve forever.

---

## Principle 5

Automation before manual work.

If software can decide,

software should decide.
