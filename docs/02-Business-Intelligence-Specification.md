# ANNEXE AI V5
# Business Intelligence Specification
## Version 1.0
### Document ID: AF-02

---

# 1. Purpose

Business Intelligence is responsible for understanding the client's business before engineering begins.

Its objective is to transform vague ideas into a structured BusinessBrief.

No engineering activity may begin without a completed BusinessBrief.

---

# 2. Mission

Understand the client's business problem completely before proposing technical solutions.

Business Intelligence focuses on business value—not technology.

---

# 3. Responsibilities

Business Intelligence owns:

- Requirement Discovery
- Client Interview
- Business Analysis
- Industry Analysis
- Competitor Awareness
- Scope Definition
- Budget Analysis
- Timeline Estimation
- Risk Identification
- Proposal Generation
- Client Qualification

Business Intelligence does NOT make engineering decisions.

---

# 4. Inputs

Business Intelligence accepts:

- Client conversation
- Uploaded documents
- Existing software
- Business goals
- Budget
- Timeline
- Constraints

---

# 5. Outputs

Business Intelligence produces:

BusinessBrief v1

This becomes the official input for Engineering Brain.

# 7. Discovery Engine

The Discovery Engine identifies:

- Business type
- Industry
- Target users
- Revenue model
- Existing systems
- Current pain points
- Desired outcomes

---

# 8. Interview Engine

Interview Engine asks intelligent follow-up questions.

Questions must reduce ambiguity.

Questions should be prioritized by impact.
