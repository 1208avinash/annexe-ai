# Roadmap RC6 → RC10
## Version 1.0
### Document ID: AF-09

---

# Purpose

This roadmap defines the planned evolution of ANNEXE AI from RC6 through RC10.

It provides strategic direction while allowing implementation details to evolve.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every release must:

- Preserve architecture
- Improve engineering intelligence
- Expand explainability
- Increase automation
- Improve quality
- Maintain backward compatibility where practical

---

# RC6 — Engineering Intelligence

Mission:

Build the Engineering Brain.

Major Deliverables:

- Requirement Intelligence
- Engineering Memory Platform
- Knowledge Engine
- Knowledge Retrieval
- Reasoning Foundation

Outputs:

EngineeringDecision v1

Success Criteria:

Engineering decisions become evidence-based.

---

# RC7 — Engineering Decision System

Mission:

Teach ANNEXE AI how engineers evaluate options.

Major Deliverables:

- Reasoning Engine
- Alternatives Engine
- Consensus Engine
- Decision Engine
- Evidence Graph

Outputs:

Explainable engineering decisions.

Success Criteria:

Every recommendation includes alternatives, evidence, confidence, and trade-offs.

---

# RC8 — Autonomous Engineering Management

Mission:

Create autonomous engineering leadership.

Major Deliverables:

- CTO AI
- Engineering Director
- Project Manager AI
- Resource Planner
- Portfolio Manager

Outputs:

Engineering management decisions.

Success Criteria:

Projects are coordinated with minimal manual intervention.

---

# RC9 — Learning Organization

Mission:

Enable continuous organizational improvement.

Major Deliverables:

- Learning Engine
- Pattern Mining
- Project DNA Optimization
- Metrics Engine
- Continuous Improvement

Outputs:

Improved engineering knowledge after every completed project.

Success Criteria:

Every completed project improves future projects.

---

# RC10 — Autonomous Engineering Company

Mission:

Operate as a complete digital engineering organization.

Major Deliverables:

- Executive AI
- Enterprise Engineering Cloud
- Autonomous Optimization
- Digital Twin
- Strategic Planning

Outputs:

End-to-end autonomous engineering lifecycle.

Success Criteria:

ANNEXE AI can execute complete engineering engagements while remaining explainable, governed, and continuously learning.
